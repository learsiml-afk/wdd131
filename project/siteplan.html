Artemis: Return to the Moon

WDD 131 · W05 Site Plan · Israel · 2026

1. Site Name

Artemis: Return to the Moon — named after NASA's active lunar program. The name is recognizable, meaningful (twin sister of Apollo in mythology), and immediately communicates the subject.

2. Site Purpose

An educational website that explains NASA's Artemis Program to a general audience — covering what it is, the hardware involved (SLS, Orion, Gateway, lunar lander), and the mission timeline from Artemis I through future crewed landings.

3. Scenarios

  • What exactly is the Artemis Program and how is it different from Apollo?
  • What rocket and spacecraft will take astronauts to the Moon?
  • When is the first crewed lunar landing scheduled to happen?
  • Why are we going back to the Moon — what's the scientific reason?

4. Color Schema

#e2e8f0 — body text
#94a3b8 — muted / captions

5. Typography

  • Georgia (serif) — headings (H1, H2, H3). Authoritative, editorial feel.
  • Palatino Linotype (serif) — body text. Warm and readable for longer content.
  • Courier New (monospace) — UI labels, dates, mission codes. Evokes NASA mission control.
